- #thoughts OpenClaw/Clawdbot/Moltbot is trending right now. It’s a personal AI assistant that has access to your computer and all your files, emails, etc and the browser. It is agentic and can do things on your behalf right in an instant messaging app. From keeping your calorie intakes to helping you update any bugs reported from a pull request in GitHub. It will find the resources based on your computer and the browser and do everything for you. Everyone is now buying a Mac mini to play with this. I don’t have the money to buy one and play with this. To be honest, I don’t have a use case for this assistant. I rarely use my laptop at home. I don’t want AI to have access to my whole life even though I told Claude about my mental health and personality and things that happened to me. AI is changing so quickly and it’s hard to keep up with. It’s stressful to know what’s the latest breakthrough with FOMO. I’m doing the opposite as I’m highly sensitive and easily get overwhelmed and sensory overload. Instead of chasing latest tech trends, I’m getting back to nature, embracing human nature to find peace and calm. I haven’t read the news for so long as it makes me pessimistic and aches me when I see bad news. News also makes me more anxious about life. All these first world problems are making us humans more and more out of touch with nature where we are most comfortable and peaceful in. These days after work, I just lie in bed and shake off my anxiety and relax. No more screen time.  Just me and my body and my brain. I sleep more these days which could also attribute to trauma processing and integration after I talked to my inner child and current self.
